"el recorrido por el pulso en la animacion svg se acelre conforme pasa entre las pulsaciones": the bright runner leaves each spike slowly and speeds up into the next, one heartbeat per stretch. "al hacer hover sobre las partes del timeline donde se muestran las obsercaviones, la frecuentcia del pulso aya cambiando, acelerandose cuando se selecciona la aprte de la alerta y descelerandoce cuando regresa a la normal". "ademas de que se active con el hover debe cambiar entre ellos automaticamente". About 6 s per state; hover, focus or tap pause it; progress bar on the active tab; no auto-advance under reduced motion.
- S7. Horizontal scroll: "desde la seccion una mañana cualquiera hasta la seccion pensada para todos los dias, sea scroll horizontal". Desktop only; mobile stays vertical for now. Sticky stage plus translateX from vertical scroll, no library, pulse progress line. Vertical fallback for reduced motion, no JS and short viewports. Morning auto-advance runs only while its panel is in view. "al llegar a la seccion \"una mañana cualquiera\" estaria interesante que el scroll no desplace rapidamente a la derecha, sino qu ehaya un espacio donde se pueda apreciar bien esa seccion y recien de ahi parsar a la derecha, y lo mismo pero sutil al finalizar el scroll horizontal": the track holds 0.9 screens on the first panel and 0.3 on the last. Menu anchors and keyboard must reach every panel. Needs a fit plan for 1366x768.

- S9. Contacts: no limit anywhere: "quita lo de los contactos porque podria parecer limitante". FAQ question becomes "¿A quién le avisa?"; its answer still has to be written.
- S10. Facts from the report (checked against the docx on 2026-11-07, L17): the band alerts you first with sound (piezo), light (LED) and its OLED screen (the page does not mention the screen, L31), and waits about 5 s for "Bien"/"Mal"; no vibration and no temperature sensor (MAX30102: pulse and SpO2). Detection runs on the band without internet; alerts go through the phone over Bluetooth LE (minimum range 10 m) and its mobile data; no WiFi. Escalation: hospital and first contact together, next contact after 10 s without a response, and a confirmation ("ayuda en camino") reaches everyone. Learning: "Estoy bien" widens the tolerance, panic narrows it. Goals: 5 s, 99,5 %, 24 h with one charge, AES-256, PIN or biometrics, Android 10+ and iOS 14+. Not in the report: IPX7 (kept as the user's nice to have, S8), 20 % warning, 2 h charge. Keep the academic disclosure in the first viewport; nothing reads as a certified or real medical device.

The old 2 MB limit is replaced by a per-screen budget, proposed: first screen about 1.25 MB desktop, 0.3 MB mobile; whole page about 2.5 MB desktop, 0.93 MB mobile. Assumption: user has not confirmed this budget.
- S14. Closing motion: "en secciones como el footer (o seccion final) se podria aprovechr la superposicon del pastito con el texto para aplicar un parallax o animacion. quedaria bien en mi opionnio y paovechariamos los recursos que tenemos".
